Cubiqua: Puzzle Adventure for Android

Place a few machine parts on a tiny floating island, press PLAY, and watch the marble roll to the golden ring. 50 handmade puzzles across six chapters, no timers, no lives.

How do I play?

Drag a part from the tray onto a dotted tile, or tap a dotted tile. Tap a placed part to turn it. Drag it back to the tray to remove it. Press PLAY to run your machine; tap again for 2× speed.

I'm stuck on a level

Tap the light bulb. The first hint shows the goal, the second points at the right tile, and the third places a part. On the first two levels every hint is free; after that, the third step asks you to watch a short video, or is always free with the Supporter Pack.

How do I get the third star?

Solve the level using no more parts than the target shown under the goal, and collect every gem if there are any.

I bought the Supporter Pack but it's not unlocked

Open Settings → Restore purchase while connected to the internet and signed in to the same Google account. Payments that are still pending unlock automatically when Google Play confirms them.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your phone automatically. It is removed if you uninstall the game or clear its data.
