Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026 · Applies to Cubiqua: Puzzle Adventure for Android (package com.cubiqua.puzzle)

In short: Cubiqua has no accounts and no sign-in, and the developer does not collect your personal information. Your progress stays on your phone. The game includes Google's ad service so that optional hint videos are ready when you ask for one; it processes some data as described below. Payments are handled by Google Play.

Who we are

Cubiqua is made by Mojahid, an independent developer ("we", "us"). Contact: raofahmedmojahid@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The game saves information such as your level progress and stars, which tutorials you have seen, how many times you have played, your settings (sound, vibration, reduced motion) and whether you own the Supporter Pack in a file on your device. This file is not sent to us or to anyone else, and Android cloud backup is turned off for this app. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

What we collect

We do not run our own servers, accounts or analytics, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information. We do not sell personal information.

Rewarded videos (Google AdMob)

Some hints can be unlocked by choosing to watch a short video. Videos are only ever shown when you tap "Watch a video"; the game never shows ads on its own. The videos are provided by Google AdMob, operated by Google LLC. So that a video is ready when you ask for one, Google's Mobile Ads SDK starts when the app opens (after your consent choice, where one is required) and loads a video in the background. While it runs, it collects and processes the following data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

DataExamples
Approximate locationEstimated from your device's IP address
Device or other identifiersAndroid advertising ID, app set ID
App interactionsApp launch, taps, video views
DiagnosticsApp launch time, hang rate, energy usage

This data is encrypted in transit. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before any ad is requested. Where US state privacy laws give you the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, you can do so in Settings → Privacy choices, which is also where everyone who was asked can change their choice. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Once the game has confirmed that you own the Supporter Pack, it shows no ads and no longer starts the Mobile Ads SDK.

Purchases (Google Play)

The optional Supporter Pack is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. The game only receives confirmation that you own the pack so it can unlock it; we never see your payment details.

Crash and performance reports

If you have allowed it in your Android settings, Google Play may share anonymous crash and performance statistics (Android vitals) with us. These reports do not identify you and help us fix problems.

Children

Cubiqua is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

Your choices and rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can control ad personalisation through Settings → Privacy choices (where offered) and your Android advertising ID settings, and you can delete all game data by uninstalling the app. For data processed by Google, use your Google account and device controls. Questions or requests: email us at the address above and we will reply within 30 days.

This website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The pages use no cookies, trackers or third-party fonts.

Security

Data sent by the Google services described above is encrypted in transit. Your save file stays on your device and is protected by Android's app sandbox.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
